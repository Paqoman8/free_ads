# FreeAds — Brand Overview

## 1. Project Mission
FreeAds is a modern, minimalistic, and user-friendly platform for posting and browsing classified ads.  
It aims to provide an intuitive and trustworthy environment where users can sell, buy, or exchange items quickly and safely.

## 2. Positioning
FreeAds positions itself as:
- Simpler and lighter than traditional classifieds websites
- Fully accessible to all users
- Clear, structured, and focused on efficiency
- Designed to inspire trust through modern UI choices

## 3. Target Audience
- Individuals selling or buying used items
- Students searching for affordable goods
- Small businesses offering services
- People looking for second-hand products quickly

## 4. Core Design Philosophy
- Prioritise **clarity** over ornamentation  
- Maintain **consistency** across all pages and components  
- Enhance **readability** through spacing and hierarchy  
- Ensure **accessibility** for all devices and users  
- Build trust with a minimal, stable, and modern interface  

## 5. Visual Attributes
- Reliable and calm (through blue tones)
- Energetic and dynamic (through orange highlights)
- Neutral and readable (through light and dark greys)
- Minimalistic and structured layouts

## 6. UX Principles
- Search-first navigation
- Fast access to filters
- High-contrast text for easy scanning
- Predictable components and behaviors
- Focus on usability before aesthetic complexity

## 7. Values Reflected in the Interface
- **Trust** → Blue palette, clean layout, predictable patterns  
- **Transparency** → Clear contrast, direct labeling, strong hierarchy  
- **Efficiency** → Simple navigation flows, minimal friction  
- **Accessibility** → Legible typography, good color contrast, mobile-first design
