# FreeAds — Logo Guidelines

## 1. Logo Concept
The FreeAds logo should be:
- Simple  
- Modern  
- Easily recognisable  
- Scalable for small sizes  

Recommended symbol:
- Megaphone → communication, announcements  
- Price tag → deals, marketplace  
- Shopping bag → commerce  

Combine the icon with the wordmark **“FreeAds”** in Inter SemiBold.

## 2. Colors
- Primary version: Blue (#1A73E8)  
- Secondary: Black/Grey for text  
- Avoid using orange for the logo (too aggressive)

## 3. Safe Zone
Maintain a minimum padding around the logo equal to:
- 0.5× the height of the text

## 4. Incorrect Usage
- Do not stretch or distort  
- Do not change the color scheme  
- Do not add shadows or outlines  
- Do not rotate the icon  

## 5. Favicon
Export the icon alone in:
- 32×32  
- 64×64  
- 128×128  

## 6. Suggested AI Prompts (optional)
“Minimal flat megaphone icon in blue (#1A73E8), geometric style, simple shapes, clean outline, suitable for logo design.”
